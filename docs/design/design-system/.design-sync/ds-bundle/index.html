Signature · the street of windows (tier 3)

The team at night. Each house is a teammate; each window is one area of their work. Lit = captured, dark = a gap. Every porch light is on. Click a house (or use the dock, or the arrow keys and Enter) and the camera flies in; the panel shows that person's work. Press Capture on a dark window to light it. Esc flies back. Without WebGL, the 2D street shows.

Illumination · warm up, then rest

When knowledge is captured, the window warms from dark to amber (600ms), breathes gently three times, then rests. Light then runs to each source, one by one.
