11 · Evidence

AI coding makes the handover problem worse

Code gets written faster, but understood less. The "why" used to live in one person's head. With AI, it often lives in a chat session that is closed and gone. The person leaving may not fully know the code either.

Atlassian State of DevEx 2025 (3,500 devs + managers)Finding information is now the #1 developer friction, ahead of technical debt. 50% lose 10+ h/week to inefficiencies. AI saves ~10 h/week, and about the same is lost to friction.The judges' own research names our problem as #1.

  • Design rule it gives us: every answer the app gives must link to its source (ticket, PR, page, interview). Developers don't trust bare AI summaries.

12 · Shape: knowledge handover

Knowledge handover: the slice map

Problem. On a project team (POs, devs, testers), what each person is working on, and why, lives in their head and is scattered across Jira tickets, chats and documents. So every absence costs time: covering someone on leave takes half a day of catching up plus messages to them on holiday, a resignation takes a week of handover that still leaves the receiver unsure, and when something breaks months later the team hunts for reasons that left with the person. AI coding makes this worse: code gets written faster, but the reasoning stays in chat sessions nobody keeps.

Acceptance scenarios

  1. Save a note and a teammate sees it. Aisha saves "Payments retry 3 times because the bank API times out under load." It appears at the top with her name and today's date; Daniel sees it later.
  2. First visit is empty. "No notes yet. Leave the first one for the next person."
  3. Empty note is not saved. "Write something first."
  4. Too long is not saved. "Notes can be up to 2,000 characters. This one is 2,001." Text stays.
  5. Save fails. "Couldn't save your note. Try again." Text stays; no duplicate after a retry.

Slice test: passes all four: every layer (page, function, table, deploy, CI); one e2e walks it; worth shipping as proven rails; 5 scenarios, 2 areas.

15 · Design system

The porch light: who is away (draft for slice porch-status)

A real porch light is left on for someone who isn't home yet. In Porchlight it is left on for the people who aren't here: their lit windows are what they left for you. If someone's porch is dark, they're here, so just ask them.

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> Here
  Here --> Away: person sets "away until date"
  Away --> Here: day after the date, automatically
  Here --> Leaving: person sets "leaving on date", or a handover starts
  Leaving --> Left: after the date, or Jira says the account is deactivated
  Here --> Left: Jira says the account is deactivated
StatusPorchSet byEnds
HereOffDefault—
Away until a dateOnThe person (lead too? O17)Automatically after the date
Leaving on a dateOnThe person, or a started handoverBecomes Left
LeftOn, stays onJira: account deactivatedNever; their knowledge stays

Never: guessing presence from activity ("quiet for 5 days"). That watches people. Open: may a lead set it (O17) · can we read deactivated accounts (O18) · does Atlassian expose out-of-office (O19) · a resignation may be confidential before it's announced (O20).

16 · Shape

Pass it on: a handover passes every window to the team

Moving house: packing every box (capturing) isn't enough. Each box needs someone at the other end who agreed to take it, and the team lead checks nobody gets buried.

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> Gap: area exists, not captured
  Gap --> Captured: interview answer saved
  Captured --> Proposed: leaver proposes a receiver
  Proposed --> Accepted: receiver accepts
  Proposed --> Captured: receiver declines
  Accepted --> PassedOn: team lead approves, light moves houses
  Captured --> Unowned: leaving date passes
  Gap --> Unowned: leaving date passes

A leaver's house shows a countdown and two meters, for example "14 of 20 captured · 9 of 20 passed on · 28 days left". Receiving an area adds a window to the receiver's house. Open: who is the team lead (O21) · what if the lead is the leaver or away (O22) · framing a 10-storey house (O23) · does a passed-on area keep its history (O24).

Acceptance scenarios

  1. Add a teammate. Daniel adds Aisha Rahman from a Jira user search. Everyone sees her with "Added by Daniel Tan". Adding her twice says "Aisha Rahman is already on the team."
  2. Take a teammate off. Ravi Kumar moved team; Daniel takes him off. Nothing changes in Jira, and he can be added again.
  3. Catch up on a teammate on leave. On 10 Nov, Daniel sees Aisha's 6 open tickets, her changes since 27 Oct, PAY-147 blocked by OPS-31 and flagged PAY-160, and her next 5 To Do tickets. HR-12, in a project Daniel cannot open, is not shown. "Showing only tickets you can open."
  4. Nothing in Jira. Each section says it is empty: "No open work." "No changes in the last 14 days." "No blockers." "No next steps."
  5. Jira does not answer. "Couldn't load Aisha's work from Jira. Try again." The team list still shows.

22 · Spec: porch-status

Porch status: scope and acceptance

A real porch light tells the street one thing: nobody is home right now, but they left the light on for you. Here it tells a teammate whether to read someone's catch-up, or just ask them.

stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> Here
    Here --> Away: person sets away until a date
    Away --> Here: end of that date, their time zone, or they set Here
    Here --> Leaving: person sets leaving on a date
    Leaving --> Here: they set Here before the date
    Leaving --> Left: end of the leaving date
    Here --> Left: Jira says active false
    Away --> Left: Jira says active false
    Leaving --> Left: Jira says active false
  1. Away. Aisha sets "Away until 20 Nov". Everyone sees her porch light on; only she can change it.
  2. Away ends. 21 Nov 00:00 in her time zone it turns off, or earlier if she sets Here.
  3. Leaving becomes Left. Ravi sets "Leaving on 31 Jan 2027" after the warning "Everyone on the team will see this." From 1 Feb he shows Left, for good.
  4. Deactivated is Left. Jira says Mei Ling's account is inactive: Left. If Jira leaves her out, she keeps her last status, never a wrong Left.
  5. Bad dates. "Pick today or a later date." · "Pick a date within a year."

Research: Jira has no out-of-office field (O7), so automatic Away became its own slice, 1c away-from-calendar, through Teamwork Graph. Deactivation is readable as active on the bulk user lookup, unproven for a deactivated account (O3, accepted with a safe rule).
